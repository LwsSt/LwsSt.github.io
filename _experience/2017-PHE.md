Maintained and extended ImmForm, the UK government's national vaccine ordering platform — a high-availability system relied upon by NHS trusts and GP practices across England. Took ownership of both day-to-day feature work and production reliability, ensuring the platform remained stable during critical immunisation campaigns.

Rearchitected legacy monolithic components into loosely coupled microservices using ASP.NET Core and RabbitMQ, improving fault isolation and enabling independent deployment of discrete services. This work laid the groundwork for a more resilient, scalable system that could better absorb demand spikes during national vaccination programmes.
