A web app that helps non-football fans navigate their social calendar by displaying upcoming matches as hazard warnings. Features collapsible time slots, color-coded threat levels (live/imminent/scheduled), and clickable stadium locations. Filter by month and see at a glance which days to avoid pubs, crowds, and football-saturated venues.
